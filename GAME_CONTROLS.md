# Game controls

Desktop defaults: A/D or Left/Right move; W or Up jump; S or Down crouch; Shift block; J jab; K heavy punch; L kick 1; U kick 2; I kick 3; O signature special; Esc pause/resume. In Options, click a key field and press a replacement key. F3 toggles the developer hitbox/state overlay; it is off by default.

Touch controls: hold the directional and block buttons; tap jump, crouch, attacks, special, or pause. Touch buttons support simultaneous direction and attack. Input clears when focus is lost or the match is paused.

On small screens, landscape offers more ring width. Portrait remains navigable; rotate for a wider play area. The canvas suppresses touch gestures while active. Pause also offers restart and quit; quitting requires confirmation and does not settle match rewards.
